Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 15, 2025

🔒 YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS: Tunix Music Bot is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Tunix Music Bot, we may collect the following types of information:

Data Type What We Collect Purpose
Discord IDs User IDs, Server IDs, Channel IDs, Role IDs To store settings, permissions, and preferences
Server Settings Prefix, volume, 24/7 mode, DJ roles To customize bot behavior per server
User Preferences Favorite songs, saved playlists To provide personalized features
Usage Data Commands used, songs played, timestamps To improve service and fix bugs

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Bot Functionality: To provide music playback, save settings, and manage queues
  • Personalization: To remember your favorite songs and playlists
  • Analytics: To understand usage patterns and improve the bot
  • Security: To prevent abuse and enforce our Terms of Service
  • Support: To help troubleshoot issues you may encounter

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored securely using MongoDB with encryption. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

3.1 Data Retention

  • Server settings are stored until the bot is removed from the server
  • User favorites and playlists are stored until you delete them
  • Usage logs may be retained for up to 30 days for analysis

3.2 Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data by:

  • Removing the bot from your server (deletes server settings)
  • Using the /clearfavorites command (deletes your favorites)
  • Contacting us via Discord for complete data deletion

4. Third-Party Services

Tunix Music Bot integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Discord: For bot functionality and authentication
  • YouTube/Spotify/SoundCloud: For streaming music content
  • MongoDB: For data storage (hosted securely)

Please review the privacy policies of these services for more information.

5. Children's Privacy

Tunix Music Bot is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your stored data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Objection: Object to certain data processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us on Discord.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Our website does not use cookies or tracking technologies. However, third-party services like Discord and Vercel may use their own tracking. Please refer to their privacy policies for details.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through our Discord server or via the bot itself. Your continued use of the bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Data Controller and Contact

Tunix Music Bot is operated by:

  • Developer: Tunix Team
  • Discord Support: Join Here
  • Email: privacy@tunixmusic.com
  • Data Requests: data@tunixmusic.com

10. GDPR Compliance (For EU Users)

If you are in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR:

  • Right to be informed
  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten)
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@tunixmusic.com.

By using Tunix Music Bot, you consent to this Privacy Policy.
